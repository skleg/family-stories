# Family Stories

*May every conversation become something worth keeping.*

**English** · [Русский](ru/)

These are the stories I want to keep: stories of family, identity, and the quiet ways technology has entered a life. I write them first for my children, and perhaps one day for theirs—not simply to preserve what happened, but to leave behind the questions, choices, and conversations that helped make us who we are.

If they offer something to another reader along the way, that is a gift.
